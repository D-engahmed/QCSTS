# Product overview

QCSTS is positioned as **Pharmaceutical Stability Management Cloud**: a B2B platform for organizations operating stability studies, not a general consumer app, generic ERP, full QMS or AI-first product.

Initial customers are pharmaceutical manufacturers, CMOs, QC laboratories and biotech organizations in Egypt, GCC and MENA. The product advantage is focused control of stability work: simpler than enterprise QMS, more controlled than spreadsheets, more specialized than generic LIMS and accessible to smaller regulated organizations.

The first commercial outcome is a controlled end-to-end stability workflow: organization → product → approved protocol/version → batch → study → chamber/condition → timepoints → sample pull → results/specification evaluation → signature → QA review → report → immutable history. AI is deferred until deterministic workflow, data quality, review and audit controls are established.

Commercial limits are represented as database plans/entitlements, not product code. The primary metric is active stability studies; users, sites, chambers, batches, API and storage are secondary predictable capacity metrics. Pricing in the product plan is a customer-discovery hypothesis, not a hard-coded commitment.
