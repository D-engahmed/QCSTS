## Commercial rules

1. No per-result billing.
2. Annual billing should normally provide 15–20% effective discount.
3. Plan limits are enforceable via entitlements, not hardcoded checks.
4. Historical quality data is not deleted because of billing state.
5. Pricing is a commercial hypothesis and must be validated with customer discovery.
